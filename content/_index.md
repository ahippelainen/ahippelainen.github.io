I'm a theoretical physicist at the University of Helsinki, working on a PhD. Most of my work is on holographic duality: the idea that certain strongly coupled quantum field theories are equivalent to gravitational theories in one more dimension (or can be understood with such). I use it as a computational tool for questions about strongly interacting matter, such as the structure of particles and what gauge theories do at high density.

If interested in the science I've done, check out the [research page](/research/) that goes through my papers, a [projects page](/projects) where I list some interesting stuff I or someone else has  built that I have come by, an [ideas page](/ideas/) with problems and content I have come by that  I think are interesting but don't have time to pursue right now, and a [blog](/blog/) for anything that I find interesting.
