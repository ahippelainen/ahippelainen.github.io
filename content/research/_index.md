Most of my work is on the gauge/gravity duality applied to QCD-like theories. A second, smaller strand is the statistics of inequality and general socio-econo-physical themes. After all, I am a model builder with a keen interest also in the human world around us. Papers are listed newest first. All works are published openly as preprints on arXiv, and code is available either directly from [GitHub](https://github.com/ahippelainen) or by request.
