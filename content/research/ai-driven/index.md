Everything on this page was found and written by AI agents that I set to work on problems I found myself interesting. Unlike the papers on my [research page](/research/), I can't follow these results step by step, so I don't count them as my research. None of them are refereed.

I publish them anyway, since a result that may well be correct or correct with only minor modifications does more good out in the open than in a drawer, and because I think we need to start finding ways to handle work like this. Why, and why in this form, is what [O, brave new academia!](/blog/o-brave-new-academia/) discusses, if you have time and interest to take a look at the issues in-depth; some posts before it are about what such results are worth.

The least I can do is give us humans a fair chance at digesting these kinds of results, should I "publish" them. Therefore, each AI-driven result comes with the note itself, a companion note that builds the argument up from roughly the level of an MSc or PhD in mathematics in the form of lecture notes, and the minimal code needed to rerun the checks, available openly on GitHub.
